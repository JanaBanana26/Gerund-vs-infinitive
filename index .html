📚 The quick grammar pit stop

Use a gerund (verb + -ing)

Common verbs: enjoy, finish, avoid, mind, suggest, keep

“We enjoy exploring new places.”

“She finished packing her suitcase.”

Use to + the base verb

Common verbs: want, hope, decide, plan, promise, learn

“I hope to visit California.”

“They decided to take the train.”
